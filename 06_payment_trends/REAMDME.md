## 1. Payment Type Trends (MoM YoY)
### Strong Growth Across All Payment Types
All payment methods show significant YoY growth in 2018 vs 2017 (Jan–Aug)

Growth is especially high in early months due to low 2017 base

**Example:**
Credit Card: **+890% (Jan)**, stabilizing to ~50–100% later

UPI: **+683% (Jan)** → slows to ~24% by August

Debit Card: extremely high growth (1000%+) due to very low base

**Insight**
The platform experienced rapid expansion in payment adoption, especially from 2017 to early 2018.

### Credit Card Dominates Volume
Credit card consistently has the highest order count across all months

**Example:**
Aug 2018 → ~4,883 orders (far higher than others)

**Insight:**
Credit card is the primary payment method, indicating strong reliance on digital/credit-based transactions.

### Growth Normalization Over Time
Growth rates decline steadily from early 2018 to later months:
* UPI: **683% → 24%**

* Credit Card: **890% → ~54%**

### Slight Decline in Voucher Usage
Voucher shows: **-5.29% in Aug 2018**

**Insight:**
Reduced reliance on vouchers and Possible shift toward direct payment methods


## 2. Installments vs Order Value
Majority of orders use 1 installment **(~49K orders)**, with steady drop as installments increase.

**Insight:**
Customers prefer single-payment transactions, indicating low friction for smaller purchases

**AOV increases with installments:**
* 1 installment → ~120

* 5 installments → ~183

* 10 installments → ~416

* 20+ installments → 600+

**Insight:**
Higher-value orders are strongly associated with installment-based payments.

Very high installments (15+) have: High AOV, but very Low Order Count.

Installments are used as a financing tool for expensive purchases, not for regular transactions.
