## 1. Delivery Delay vs Customer Satisfaction Score

| Delay Buckets | Average Review Score | Order Count |
|---------------|----------------------|-------------|
|    Early      |         4.3          |   	87196	   |
|   On Time	    |         4.1          |    2748     |
| 1-3 days late |         3.29         |    1856     |
| 4-7 days late	|         2.1          |    1756     |
| 7+ days late  |         1.7	         |    2797     |

* Early deliveries (~87K orders) achieve the highest rating (4.3)
* On-time deliveries slightly lower at 4.1
* Even minor delays (1–3 days) drop ratings sharply to 3.29
* Severe delays (7+ days) result in very low ratings (1.7)

**Insight:**
Customer satisfaction is highly sensitive to delivery performance. Even small delays trigger a big drop in ratings, indicating low tolerance for late deliveries.

* Review scores are consistently high across major payment types **(~4.0–4.2)**
* Debit card shows slightly higher rating **(4.17)**, but on low volume.
* Credit card & UPI dominate volume with similar ratings **(~4.09)**

**Insight:**
