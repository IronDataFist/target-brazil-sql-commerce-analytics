## 1. State-Level Delivery Performance
### Delivery time varies significantly across states:
  * **Slowest**: Roraima (RR): ~29 days; Amapá (AP): ~27 days; Amazonas (AM): ~26 days

  * **Fastest**: São Paulo (SP): ~8.3 days; Paraná (PR): ~11.5 days; Minas Gerais (MG): ~11.5 days

**Insight:**

Delivery time is strongly influenced by geographic remoteness and infrastructure, with northern states taking ~2–3x longer than major economic regions like SP.

### All states show negative delivery delay, meaning:
Orders are generally delivered before the estimated date

* Example: Amapá (AP): -18.7 days; Amazonas (AM): -18.6 days; São Paulo (SP): -10.1 days

**Insight:** 

Delivery estimates are consistently overestimated, providing a buffer across all regions.
